This is an aliquot. In a lab, an aliquot is a small, measured portion drawn from a larger sample and studied as if it stands for the whole. This site is one such portion of a living project, taken at a single moment, not the project itself. Everything here was assembled from a June 2026 archive of the Hello You Experiment across X, Substack, Consorted 2026, and other public artifacts, covering October 2025 through June 2026, then run through a deliberately unoptimized AI analysis to map patterns, synthesize results, and interpret the project. The writing is lightly edited, and offline material is not included.

Read it as a snapshot dated June 2026, not as fact. It will contain mistakes and AI misreadings, and it describes framings that were true when the posts were written but that the author may no longer hold, or that later HYE content has since changed. Its purpose is not to be correct but to show one holistic AI interpretation of the project at a single stage, so its evolution can be seen and a later version measured against it. In the project's own language, this is one phenotype of the material at a specific time, not the full genotype.

How It Fits Together

One mind, worked across many substrates.

A lossy map of the Hello You Experiment, weighted to the record anyone can verify from the outside. It is a way to see the shape of the whole thing at once, and to see where it points next.
Built from the public spine: the Substack book, the Medium series, the LinkedIn posts and articles, Consorted 2026, the website, and the sealed X archive. Read cold, then assembled. Lossy by design, because the record is larger than any single reading of it.
The spine in one paragraph

What actually happened, in the order it happened.

A scientist trained in biochemistry, genetics, and neuroscience became a designer, then led design for two and a half years at a profitable inventory company. The work environment separated what was said from what was true, and the author began documenting it from the inside, first as vision drift, later as a set of communications engineered to read one way while employed and a second way after leaving. The role ended abruptly during a leadership transition in July 2025. That October, alone for four days, the author rebuilt the mental load of that environment as a private workflow to test whether the adaptation would return. Four days became roughly six months of recombination. Everything public since is the residue of that period, sorted into lanes.

Weighting. The public artifacts are the authoritative spine because they are verifiable outside the author. The private material in Notion, Obsidian, the working Figma files, and the early ChatGPT log is real and often clarifying, but it sits underneath the spine as raw supporting evidence, not as the record itself.
The higher level concept

Three selves, working one subject.

Underneath the project is one person running three inquiries at once, one for each professional identity, each aimed at a different face of the same question, how meaning, environment, and communication shape human beings under long exposure. The Hello You Experiment is where the three converge. It is rough, and it is a hypothesis rather than settled fact, but it is the higher level concept the rest of the work hangs from.

The science self studied intergenerational cultural inheritance, and its output is the biocultural manuscript. The design self studied behavioral social communication and visual influence, and its output is the codon method, Sortish, Inverse Perception Design, and the Y series, which is a user flow built on the author himself. The psychology self studied how environmental conditions and communication change psychological states under long exposure, and its output is the occupational mental health essay. Read apart they look like three unrelated bodies of work. Read together they are one argument approached from three directions, which is why the substrates that follow keep saying the same thing in different languages.

The shape

A single argument, fed by many lanes.

Every substrate carries the same core claim at a different level of exposure. The public lanes flow into the argument. The private lanes feed the public lanes but stay beneath the surface. The X archive runs alongside as a sealed channel, the safe place where the writing style was developed while hiding in the noise.

The engine

Three lenses, welded by drift.

The whole body of thought triangulates against three thinkers, each naming a different layer of reality. Darwin is the descriptive layer, natural selection, what is happening to cognition under pressure. Bernays is the mechanism layer, manufactured consent, what is being done to people through the engineering of belief. Dada, by way of Hugo Ball, is the response layer, chosen incoherence, what to do when the rational machinery starts producing something monstrous. Each lens has its own drift, and the drift is the point. Meaning is allowed to slide so that new meaning can form.

The scientific spine

Underneath the art, a formal paper.

The science self's track has a rigorous core, a working manuscript titled A Biocultural Feedback Framework for the Intergenerational Transmission of Trauma. It is a hypothesis and theory paper, not an essay, with an abstract, a coupled dynamical model written as equations, a research agenda, a limitations section, and roughly twenty peer reviewed citations verified against the literature. The paper calls itself a hypothesis, and it folds into the project as one of the three tracks rather than as a finished proof. Its argument is that a class of persistent societal problems is held in place by a loop between population stress physiology and shared cultural meaning. Adverse experience is transduced through the neuro immune endocrine axis into epigenetic settings on the stress axis, a population tuned toward threat preferentially adopts and passes on narratives of fear, and those narratives rebuild the environment that produced the stress. The reproducing unit is the loop, not the gene and not the idea, held together by reconstruction rather than copying.

The coupled biology and culture loop, rather than either the gene or the idea, is the unit that reproduces, with the epigenetic setpoint of the stress axis as its most stable physical anchor.

Two things make this the anchor of the whole spine. It is the most verifiable artifact in the project, because its links are checkable in the published record, which is exactly the property the author trusts. And it names the shapes the personal work only points at. The model predicts hysteresis, a system that locks into a bad state and stays there after its original cause is gone, which is the precise structure of the adaptation that followed the author home and took months to fade. The private experiment is a single case of the public framework, and the word transduction, used here for experience becoming a molecular signal, is the scientific parent of the artistic transduction in the method below.

The method stack

How one thought becomes many artifacts.

The same pipeline runs under everything, from a single tweet to a 55 minute film. It is why the work feels both scattered and unified.

cogLang and Sortish. A private, deliberately multi stable writing style. Clarity goes in, ambiguity comes out, so the same sentence can hold more than one true reading.
Codon triangulation. Each unit carries three anchors, a cognitive thread, an emotional charge, and a cultural reference, usually as text plus emoji plus a GIF. Three points are enough to reconstruct the original state later.
Transduction. A fixed idea is forced into a form it was never meant to occupy, a poem, pseudocode, a recipe, a fake newscast. If the meaning survives the translation, it was a real structure.
Inverse Perception Design. A message engineered to read one way in the present and a second, truer way once the environment shifts. The performance review, the team video, the farewell, the Glassdoor review, the colored shirts.
Cold read, or phenotype. A fresh model with no memory is handed one artifact and asked only to interpret it. The artifact is the genotype and stays fixed. Each reading is a phenotype and varies by reader. The variance is the finding, not an error.
Why post at all

Social media, run in reverse.

The public posting works nothing like broadcasting. Social media is turned inside out here and used as a research journal, a place to think rather than a channel for reach, so a post is there to be questioned and the likes are beside the point. Seen that way the X archive stops looking like a feed and starts reading like a lab notebook someone left open on the internet.

Public exposure is the instrument. Putting an unfinished thought in front of strangers manufactures a real fear that it will be misread, and that fear is used on purpose. The tension is what pushes the author to evolve the writing later and close the gap, a self directed conditioning loop rather than a bid for an audience. It reads like McLuhan turned inside out, where the act of posting, and not its content, is the mechanism doing the work.

This post exists publicly for you to question, designed to cause me fear that you will misinterpret it and change your perception of me, and for that tension to push me into evolving my communication style later to anneal the gap.

It is built to resist the metrics it runs on. Nothing is optimized for virality, karma, or engagement, which is why the Reddit experiments describe karma as something that conflicted with anti incentive communication, and why the standalone essay on chirality argues that virality was the wrong goal all along. Hiding in the noise is deliberate. The camouflage of the feed buffers the audience pressure, so the exposure stays real while remaining survivable. That combination is what made X the safe place to grow Sortish in public, a diary written where everyone could see it and almost no one would look.

Each platform then carries a different job. X holds the raw logs and the codons and is the primary research log. Medium holds the transduction artifacts, the ideas that left a mechanism in one discipline and reappeared as art in another. Reddit runs the persona experiments. GitHub holds the algorithmic transduction. TikTok was meant to hold the human story and was set private because it was too personal. Figma is the substrate underneath all of it, the surface where the racing thoughts landed first before they were sorted into lanes.

The hinge

The experiment that studies itself.

The Y-series is where the project folds back on its own hand. Y.1 is the method, the four day setup, the closed loop learning, the sound therapy inversion, the cancer deadline used as a synthetic failure mode. Y.2 is the cost, the four days becoming six months, the adaptation returning on its own and following the author home to the dinner table. Y.3 is the finding, and it doubles as the request that gives the whole map its purpose.

The environment I built to study what workplaces do to people did what workplaces do to people. What I am asking for is interpretation from people who bring a lens I don't have.

That sentence is why the public spine matters more than the private notes. The author cannot fully read the artifacts alone, because looking at them reactivates the state that made them. The reading has to come from outside.

The through line

One claim under all of it.

Cheap artifacts let organizations reach traction before they form meaning, so meaning gets manufactured backward from success. Systems built to scale replace one specific intention with a more common one, sorted by frequency, and call the averaging alignment. The people closest to the work absorb the incoherence privately, and the cost shows up in the body before it shows up in any dashboard. The argument is not against business. It is against the idea that business as usual is the highest form of organization a person can imagine. The purple path is the refusal to be averaged down to fit.

Where the map points

The next step.

As a record the work is finished. As an argument it is still open, which is the exact state Y.3 names when it asks to be read from the outside. Four directions are already sitting in the material, waiting.

The mental health essay is the way in. Of everything gathered here it is the one document a stranger can read cold, cited and checkable, with no insider knowledge required. It is the door, and the rest of the work hangs off its frame.

The spine wants to become a single path. The substrates already lean the same way, so a guided route from the front door through the book to the film would carry a reader through the whole thing without ever handing them the private notes.

The interpretation is the experiment. The move from genotype to phenotype is the research itself. Opening the cold read to many people and treating the spread of readings as the data keeps the experiment alive rather than closing it.

The X lane stays sealed. It remains beside the rest as the ground where the language was grown, referenced but never folded in, so the noise it hides inside stays whole.

One honest caveat, offered in the project's own spirit. This map is lossy, and the record is larger than any single reading of it. There is more the author has not surfaced here. What this map shows is one phenotype among many, never the genotype itself.
