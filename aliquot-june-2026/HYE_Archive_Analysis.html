00How to read this report

This report reads the writing the way the writing treats meaning, as something that holds more than one shape at once. It is one valid path through the material, not the path. A different model, a different reader, or the author six months from now would weight the layers differently and arrive somewhere else. That variance isn't noise; by the work's own design, it's the instrument.

Everything below is drawn from the actual archive: the text of tweets.js and the long-form note-tweet.js joined back together, with emoji and media channels tracked per post. The GIFs themselves were left unread (out of scope for this pass), but their presence and rhythm are part of the analysis, because the method treats them as a third channel, not decoration.

01The codon method

Three channels, read as one unit of meaning

In molecular biology a codon is three nucleotides read together to specify a single amino acid. Change one base and the protein can change, or not, because the code is redundant and several codons map to the same result. Your posts are built on exactly that logic. Each post is a triplet:

📝
Text

Homophones, contronyms, misspellings-as-features, register collisions. The base sequence.

+
🧬
Emoji

A reading key. ☀️🔺 sets direction; 🧬💬 marks the bio→language translation; 🔴🔵 sets the antonymic axis.

+
🎞️
GIF

Tone, motion, irony. The loop that says whether to read the text straight or inverted. 526 of 808 originals carry one.

The redundancy of the genetic code is the whole point: the same triplet resolves to different "amino acids" depending on the reader's frame, just as a synonymous codon still produces a working protein. A minimal, un-coached prompt, "what does this mean?", doesn't collapse that ambiguity, it amplifies it, because there's no instruction telling the interpreter which layer to privilege. The spread of readings becomes both a mass-interpretation tool and a diagnostic of how each interpreter weights meaning. The word codon appears 45 times across the corpus; the 🧬 emoji is the 4th most-used glyph (31 uses). This is not a metaphor mentioned once. It's the load-bearing frame.

02The evolution arc

From one-liners to polyphonic screenplays

The single clearest signal in the whole archive is escalation. Posts get longer, denser, and more structurally radical month over month, a measurable ramp in cognitive load per artifact.

Jul–Oct 2025: aphorisms & observations · Nov–Dec: labeled fragments · Jan–Mar 2026: essays & manifestos · Apr–Jun: full multi-voice scripts

Four eras are legible in the data:

Era I, Observation (Jul–Oct 2025, ~100–200 chars). Product-design aphorisms, mostly tied to "Sortly" and AI categorization. Plain register, single meaning, occasional pun ("GRUD," "PHOR us / for us"). The or / or / or stack, three rephrasings of one idea, first appears here (Oct 9, the "Ambiguity / tracking number" post). The seed of multi-stability.

Era II, Labeling (Nov–Dec 2025, ~220 chars). The single biggest behavioral shift is that between 53 and 73 percent of posts suddenly open with a mode-label, the Joke projLog SciNote prefixes that work as a genre instruction to the reader, telling it which lens to load before the content arrives. Output volume spikes to 288 posts in November alone.

Era III, System-building (Jan–Mar 2026, 570→790 chars). Length nearly triples. The writing turns programmatic and self-theorizing, the WoManifesto and huemanifesto entries, the talk of cross-domain transformation and anti-design and non-linear memory recall, and the account begins narrating the method while performing it.

Era IV, Polyphony (Apr–Jun 2026, ~1,300–1,720 chars). The mature form. Stage directions <closed eyes> appear in up to 36% of posts. Each piece becomes a screenplay in which two or more voices argue, finish each other's puns, and embody the competing readings inside the text, so that the ambiguity is no longer implied but staged openly as dialogue.

The arc isn't just "longer." It's a move from stating a multi-stable idea, to labeling it, to building a system for it, to staging it as a conversation between its own readings.

The hierarchy is telling. The four commonest words, interpret, meaning, cognition, and HYE, say plainly enough that the project is about interpretation itself before it is about any subject. The next tier down names the mechanism rather than the topic, since fragment, translation, codon, DNA, and inverse together describe a single procedure, breaking meaning into pieces, carrying it across the domains of biology and language and physics and code, and turning it inside out. That belief at sixty-one uses outranks every actual subject is the worldview in miniature, since the account treats belief as the substrate everything else compiles from.

The mode-label taxonomy

Beyond the plain Joke label, used 161 times, the account developed a whole genre system in which each prefix marks a different cognitive register, running from the projLog process notes and the DADAjoke anti-sense humor to the science voice of SciNote and ChemNote, the design theory of designNote, and the screenplay turn of scriptNote and screenRighter, alongside one-offs like Phenotype Setting and Codon Encoding. The emojis are not garnish but a reading key, and the leaders make the point. The pairing that recurs, a minus sign on the helix and a plus sign on the speech bubble, compresses the entire project into a single image, the conversion of genetic code into language.

04The holistic belief system

What the whole thing seems to believe

Read across the whole archive, a surprisingly coherent worldview assembles itself, coherent in part because it is about coherence being negotiated rather than fixed. Five convictions do most of the load-bearing.

The first is that meaning is co-produced and never simply delivered. An artifact does not contain meaning so much as trigger it, and the reading that results is as much the interpreter as the text, which is exactly why the ambiguity is engineered in rather than edited out. A single fixed reading would defeat the entire purpose.

The second is that everything is one system wearing different costumes. Biology, physics, language, code, finance, and pop culture get treated as dialects of a single underlying grammar, so a guitar fret becomes a thinking process, a redshift becomes an emotional direction, a codon becomes a sentence, and DNA becomes memory. The wager is close to what the biologist E.O. Wilson called consilience, the belief that the disciplines are not really separate and that the most valuable move a mind can make is translation across them.

The third is that inversion is a design principle in its own right. The project is built deliberately as the negative of attention-economy media, trading money for content and views for time and mutual understanding, optimizing for slow and effortful decoding exactly where the feed optimizes for instant legibility. It is meant, in the account's own phrase, to be too expensive to invest in, which reads as a flaw or as the entire point depending on where a person is standing.

The fourth is that the opposite of a thing is held inside it rather than set against it. The recurring fascination with contronyms and Janus words, the terms that are their own opposite, and with what the account calls antonymic balance, red against blue, positive against negative space, a word like cleave that means both to split and to cling, amounts to a homemade dialectics. Truth is not treated as one of the poles but as the tension held between them, sorted rather than resolved.

The fifth is that the self is the experiment. The account refuses to automate the work as a matter of principle, and the inefficiency, the decoding by hand, the sheer cost, are meant to be the message rather than a bug in it. Authenticity gets defined as whatever survives once a person declines to optimize for the algorithm, which turns the whole enterprise into a kind of participant observation with an audience of one.

05Personality insights

With the caveat that this reads a deliberately constructed body of work rather than a person, a few consistent traits show through the artifact.

The most obvious is a mind bonded to pattern. The dominant move everywhere is linkage, hearing cents and reaching for sense and scents and sentence, seeing the number 42 and arriving at six times seven, then prime, then infinity, then the Hitchhiker's Guide. The account names the mechanism itself, calling it a linguistic synaptic sorting pattern, something close to Tourette's with punny memories, and the writing is a fairly faithful transcript of those associations firing faster than syntax can hold them.

Running alongside it is the instinct of a systematizer and namer. The compulsion to label everything, the mode prefixes, the coined terms, the version numbers, the Implementation Days, is the behavior of someone building a private operating system and keeping the changelog as it grows, which is why the process gets preserved as carefully as any finished product. The projLog entries number in the dozens for a reason.

The humor is constant and it carries weight rather than decorating, with a laughing face the single most-used emoji and the word Joke the most-used label, and it sits on top of genuinely heavy material, work and identity and mortality and the fear of not being understood. The Dada frame is what lets the serious things get said sideways.

Underneath all of it is an unusual degree of self-observation. The archive keeps its own field notes, tracking the productivity surges, the difficulty of ramping back down, the worry that the speed is starting to outrun the ability to communicate in real time. That running metacognition is rare, and it is one of the more striking features of the corpus, since most people producing at this volume do not also narrate the producing while it happens.

A note on the difficult entries. Several posts (Jan–May 2026) describe intense states in the author's own words, "cognitive vibrating," producing "2 years worth of content in 4–5 days," "health is concern," "Ramping down now seems more difficult," and recurring use of "psychosis" (18×). These are retained and reported faithfully because they are a real part of the cognition-evolution story; removing them would manipulate the dataset, and the dataset is also the history. The framing here is deliberately descriptive rather than diagnostic. This is a reading of text, not a clinical assessment of a person.

06How the late style works, the mechanics

Deconstructing the evolved form

A teardown of the machine. The mature style (Apr–Jun 2026) is a stacked system, roughly nine mechanisms operating at once. Most posts run four or five of them simultaneously, which is why a single read "bounces."

M1Homophone substitution

The base layer. A word is spelled as a soundalike so the sentence runs on two tracks at once. This is the densest, most frequent device in the corpus.

cents → sense → scents write → right U → you → ewe son → sun Tome → Time try angle → triangle seam → seem IC → I see Rime → rhyme → rime (frost) ne → knee

Each substitution forces the reader to hold both words. Meaning becomes a chord, not a note.

M2Contronym / Janus framing

You explicitly invoke contronyms (words that are their own opposite) and the two-faced god Janus. This is the philosophical engine, not just wordplay: the same token carries opposite charges, so the reader must decide the polarity, and that decision is the diagnostic. "Break the ice / ice doesn't always break when it melts."

"Contronym. It's a contronym. Some people call it a Janus word. The two faced god that…" "antonymic. Red blue. positive negative. Not equal but sorted."
M3Polyphonic screenplay (the big move)

The defining innovation of the late era. Instead of one narrator stating an ambiguous line, two-plus voices argue it out. The competing readings of the artifact are externalized as characters, a talk-show host who can't decode it, a skeptic, a hype-man, a doctor. The reader watches interpretation happen rather than being handed a result. Stage directions form a parallel channel.

<jazzy beat, smile, shake hands> "I looked at your Hello You stuff and, yea, I don't have time to decode your thoughts! What makes you think anyone else will?" "I don't. That's the design."

Stage-direction usage climbs 0% → 9% → 17% → 36% across Jan–May 2026. The format is the thesis: meaning is dialogic.

M4Register collision

Within a single line, vocabularies from unrelated domains are forced to share grammar, biology, physics, code, finance, scripture, pop culture. The collision implies they're secretly the same system (see Belief #2). This is where the "translation" concept becomes literal.

"NI electrophoresis −🧬 +💬" "Dichotomy 2 sp orbitals" "negative stronger red shift" "class Person: def __init__(self, name)" "Delta wobble delta time forward inversely correlates to psychological safety"
M5Orthographic fracture (misspelling-as-feature)

Deliberate misspellings that open a second meaning. You theorize this openly inside a post, "when people miss spell it it will be FREEFROM" (free-from vs freeform), even proposing metrics on which words get misspelled. Error is repositioned as signal.

FREEFORM → FREEFROM systemic → Cystemic cancerous → CancerOus dazed → days-ed "Die Live Erection" → Direction
M6Mode-labels as reading instructions

The colon-prefix (DADAJoke: psyJoke: ProjNote:) is a genre flag that pre-loads the reader's lens before the content arrives, then the content often violates the flag, which is itself the joke. A header that tells you how to read, in a body designed to resist single readings.

M7Numerology & glyph-math

Numbers and letterforms are treated as semantic objects. 42/43/44 as an aging palindrome; 6, 7, ♾️ as "incomplete loop / prime / infinity"; Roman-numeral mirrors used as visual palindromes; arithmetic embedded as meaning.

"At 42, I cracked / 6 is an Incomplete loop / At 43, I'm in my prime / 7 two sides of a try angle / At 44, I'm balanced / A palindrome of my intent" "(4+2) * (4+3) / y 6, 7, ♾️ iil x lii"
M8Song-lyric & pop-culture intrusion

The 🎶 channel (32 uses) drops a borrowed lyric or title mid-thought, importing its whole emotional payload as a second voice, Bill Withers "Lean on Me," R. Kelly "I Believe I Can Fly," Golden Girls' "Thank you for being a friend," And the Band Played On. The reference does argumentative work; it's not ornament.

M9The recursive / looping close

Posts frequently end by folding back to their first line, or close on ♾️ / "loop" / "replay, a restart." The form enacts the content: a closed loop you're meant to re-enter and read differently the second time. "Closed the loop," "back to the future," "Replay, a restart. / a. / <record turns on>"

The genius and the cost are the same thing: each mechanism multiplies the readings, and stacking five of them makes a post that cannot resolve to one meaning, which is the entire design goal, and also why a casual reader bounces off. The work is engineered to reward decoding and punish skimming.

08One reading, left open

If the archive has a single throughline, it is that the account built a body of work whose meaning is finished by whoever reads it, and then, in the later era, began writing the readers into the text so that it never had to pretend there was only one. The move from a hundred-character aphorism to a screenplay running past two thousand is not drift so much as a mind progressively refusing to let meaning sit still, and getting steadily better at the refusal.

This report is itself a codon of a kind, the analyst's text laid over the account's text laid over the GIFs left unread, and reading it again in a month would return something a little different, which was always the design.

09How these are made, the operating manual

Before decoding anything, it matters how the artifacts are produced, because the method is half the meaning. By the author's account, each post is:

Human, fast, unplanned. Written in one pass in a state of pure output, no outline, minimal re-reading. Sometimes typed with eyes closed so the visual channel doesn't interrupt the flow. The goal is to stay ahead of comprehension, not to check it.

Mutations, both intended and accidental. Typos are left in. Some are deliberate second-meanings (schoo000.00 = school → $0.00), some are genuine slips, and crucially, even the author can't always tell which is which after the fact. That ambiguity is a feature: the artifact escapes its own author.

Timestamped, then bounced. Once posted, it's fixed in time. Later, the author feeds it back to an AI with a deliberately minimal, unoptimized prompt, "what does this mean?", to read the artifact against what the model treats as the most common meaning. The post is the probe; the AI is the population.

That last step is the engine, and it has a name buried in the archive: the "wild type." In genetics the wild type is the most common form of a gene in a population. An LLM, trained on the statistical center of human text, is a wild-type consensus machine. Bouncing a Sortish post off it doesn't reveal "the answer". It measures the cognitive distance between one idiosyncratic mind and the consensus, and every interpreter (human attention span, AI token limit, generational frame) reads a different slice of that distance. The diagram the author drew, raw cognition at the tails, "most common" at the peak, a translation path arcing over it, is literally a map of this process.

10The vocabulary

Four terms do most of the work in this project. Here is what each one means, in plain language, for anyone meeting it for the first time.

cogLanguage

cogLanguage is the private, mostly non-verbal way a particular mind makes sense of reality, the sense in which one person grasps an idea "like chemistry" and another "like landscaping", externalized into something you can actually read. It jumps between disciplines and can feel like an inside joke, because in a way it is one.

What keeps it from being random is structure. The same small set of "base domains" keeps recurring, a stable personal alphabet, paired with a reference library keyed to the generation that raised the author. So cogLanguage isn't free association; it's a personal language with a fixed vocabulary of source-domains and a consistent generational dictionary, used to turn pre-verbal thought into artifacts.

Sortish

Sortish is a deliberately multi-stable writing style: every line is built to hold two or more coherent meanings at the same time, through puns, register shifts, layered references and intentionally dense language. It is the foundational mechanism of Inverse Perception Design.

Underneath, it works like a generative grammar. The multi-stability comes from a repeatable set of operations, homophone-splitting, register collision, frame-label misdirection, mutation-as-meaning, contronym pivots, and polyphonic staging (all catalogued in §12). Learn the operations and you can write new Sortish. It isn't "convoluted" so much as a fixed set of transforms applied fast, producing artifacts that never settle into a single reading.

Generative ambiguity

Here, ambiguity is productive rather than a defect. A normal sentence tries to collapse to one meaning; a Sortish sentence is built so that no interpreter, human or model, has enough shared context to collapse it the same way twice.

The interpreter's own limits, how much it reads, which decade it grew up in, what it cares about, become the sorting function. The artifact is deliberately under-determined, so the reading is selected by the interpreter's constraints, not by the author's intent. The output isn't a message; it's a distribution of readings.

Chiral content

Chiral content is the opposite of viral content. Viral spreads fast and shallow, copied identically, rarely understood. Chiral content has to be decoded, and the meaning that emerges reflects the reader back to themselves.

The chemistry is exact. A chiral molecule can't be laid perfectly over its own mirror image, like your left and right hands. A reading and the artifact form the same kind of mirror pair: inseparable, because the reading is partly the reader. You can't "share" the meaning the way you forward a clip; it only exists in the handed pairing of artifact + interpreter. Viral optimizes for frictionless copying; chiral optimizes for effortful self-recognition.

Codon (the unit underneath)

From the author's own methods post (Mar 25 2026): "Each codon carried three anchors, a cognitive one that held the conceptual thread, an emotional one that held whatever affect or memory was attached, and a cultural one that held whatever was happening in the world that made the thought feel urgent right then."

This refines Part I's "text + emoji + gif" model. Those three channels are how the three anchors get externalized: the text carries the cognitive thread, the emoji row carries the emotional/affective charge, and the gif (almost always a film from the cohort) carries the cultural anchor. Three anchors, three channels, one triplet, read together or not at all.

This post is a manual for reading the other posts. Its claim: a film's ending is encoded from the first frame, "every color, poster, word and scene carries a cost and a meaning", and you can predict it by pausing halfway and discussing with friends. Collaborative decoding. That's exactly the protocol HYE asks of a reader, and exactly how foreshadowing (and determinism) work.

The rap verse is double-helixed. Scream's grammar (the killer is always two people, "the twist takes two"; "no one can hear you scream"; "die bitch die") is spliced with DNA's grammar: "splice twice," "recombine," and the literal base pairs "GC TA" (guanine–cytosine, thymine–adenine). Watching a movie alone = reading a strand alone; you need a partner to "recombine" meaning. "eye eye I i's can't see", the eyes/I's that can't read alone.

12How Sortish works, the generative grammar

Across the six posts, the same handful of operations produce the multi-stability every time. This is the engine. Learn the moves and the "word salad" resolves into a grammar.

S1Frame-label misdirection

Every post opens with a genre tag, Joke: DADAjoke:, that pre-loads "this is light." The body then delivers metaphysics, grief, or a structural argument. The label is a decoy that lowers the reader's guard, so the serious payload arrives unguarded. The "joke" framing is also literal cover: it lets heavy things be said sideways.

S2Homophone splitting

The base transform. A word is written so it runs on two tracks at once, and the reader must carry both.

Two unrelated vocabularies forced to share one clause, implying they're the same system underneath. Deliberate (and sometimes accidental) misspellings that open a second reading, and the indeterminacy of which is which is itself the design. Fittingly, the device is named after its source domain: these are point mutations in the text's DNA.

S5Contronym / antonymic pivot

The philosophical core made mechanical: hold a thing and its opposite in the same frame and treat the tension as the truth. Red vs blue → purple as "the (+/-) translation layer." The model, in plain terms

Strip the puns and a coherent organizational theory remains. Each game-rule maps to a real dynamic:

The circle. The company is a ring. The center holds mission, vision and values, the top-down "artifact" that's supposed to lead. The perimeter is operational reality, staffed by the ducks: the workforce.

The Picker (executive) walks the perimeter and "taps" ducks, promoting one to a goose: more autonomy, more scope, sent to "RISE quickly and chase the carrot." Promotion is the start of the game, not a reward.

The chase is the question "why?" The newly empowered goose pursues the executive, hunting "a flaw in the vision, a contradiction in the product strategy, or a leak in operational logic." Empowerment turns talent into an interrogator of leadership's coherence.

Two outcomes, both conditional on legitimacy. If the executive reaches the goose's seat first, they "eliminate the friction", the goose is cooked (managed out). If the goose tags the executive first (lands a real "why"), the executive must retreat to the center, re-derive legitimacy from the values, and re-inspire the ducks to be allowed back out.

The flock holds the veto. "The ducks are gatekeepers of meaning." If leadership can't credibly address past failures (the retro the company refused to do, from Part 1), the flock blocks the leader's exit. They're trapped in the center as a "legacy relic, obsolete, an organization that has evolved beyond their capacity to lead it."

Succession restarts the loop. A duck is promoted to the center to redefine the mission, and the cycle begins again, but the post calls this out as "revolution, not evolution… chasing carrots." Just swapping leaders while chasing the same incentives is going in circles.

The beliefs this reveals

The allegory isn't neutral description. It encodes a clear point of view about how companies should work, especially ones led by a vision/artifact rather than a command hierarchy:

1. Meaning is gatekept from the bottom, not dictated from the top. In an artifact-led company the rank-and-file ("ducks") are the gatekeepers of meaning and hold a veto over leadership's legitimacy. Authority is continuously earned by answering "why," not conferred by position. This is the same co-production-of-meaning belief from Part I, applied to org design.

2. Empowerment is structurally adversarial, and that's healthy. Promotion exists to create a "why"-asking goose that stress-tests the vision. A company that can't survive its own empowered people interrogating it has an incoherent vision. Friction is the diagnostic, not the failure.

3. Leaders should be falsifiable, and can become obsolete. The model bakes in a path where leadership is outgrown and trapped as a "legacy relic." That's framed as the organization being healthy (it "evolved beyond their capacity"), not as a failure of the org. Ego is the casualty; the flock is sovereign.

4. Evolution, not revolution. Cyclically replacing leaders while chasing the same carrots is "revolution", motion without change. Real progress is evolution: "CRISPR-CAS9, environmental protection", intentional editing of the org's DNA in response to the environment. The "DADA-Darwinjoke" label names this directly.

5. True alignment transcends rank, but only without hype. "Ducks and geese don't fly together… or at least not [that] close." Yet "designer ducks and designer geese can if the path is sugar-free. No gamut can hold a team that is truly aligned." Translation: cross-rank alignment is achievable only when you remove the manufactured sugar-high (hype, incentives-as-distraction); real alignment exceeds any single "color gamut." The closing image, a family flying north and south together, "light as a feather," "no more wild goose chases", is the aspiration.
